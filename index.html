Welcome to Ember Nest

Your first goal: gather five eggs and grow your first Hatchling.

  1. Gather. Each egg costs 1 energy. Energy refills automatically; watch the timer beside it.
  2. Merge. Drag matching stages onto each other to stack them. Five become the next dragon; any leftovers stay on the board.
  3. Grow. Merges earn coins and XP. Tap fogged tiles to buy more space, and complete weekly contracts for rewards. Auto Merge unlocks at level 5.

Roost: Place dragons on perches to earn coins each minute. Visit the Dragon Bank to collect them. More rooms unlock more perches.

Nest: Buy décor to increase merge coin rewards and unlock rooms. Select an unlocked room to change your nest theme.

Ash Trial: Start with 13 gathers and two safe merges. Merge beside ash to burn it; Young and Hearth dragons clear a row, and Elders clear a row and column. Clear 8 ash to win. After two merges, each promotion adds 2 ash. Reaching 10 ash, having no room for new ash, or exhausting your pouch with no five-merge available ends the run. Your home dragons stay safe.

Hatchery upgrades: Discover Young for a 20% chance of gathering a Hatchling. After Hearth, gathers give Hatchlings with a 20% chance of a Wyrmling. Each gather costs 1 energy. Energy refills every 8 seconds, even while away, up to your capacity.

Weekly contracts: Five goals replace the old nest quests and five-gift milestone. They reset Monday at midnight in your local time. Each week's difficulty is fixed when generated. Replace one unclaimed contract per week for free (its progress is lost). Claim all five for +500 coins and a cosmetic token. In the contracts panel, claim Ember Crown free or spend one token each on Tide Crown and Grove Crown. Equip or unequip these frames from The Stash. Sleepy Dragon remains a separate daily offering.

Shopping: New décor unlocks with dragon discoveries and adds percentage bonuses. Each room purchase opens one fog tile. Shop prices rise gently for the first ten purchases each day, then stop rising; the next day resets prices.

Daily rewards: The first three trial clears each day pay rewards. After your first victory, Blitz claims a remaining daily clear instantly.

Home & Stash: Click a cosmetic to equip it. Click its EQUIPPED tile again to take it off.
